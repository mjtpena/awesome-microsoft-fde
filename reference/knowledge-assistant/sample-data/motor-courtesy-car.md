# Motor courtesy car policy

Status: current
Version: 1
Groups: all-staff

## Who gets a courtesy car

A courtesy car is provided only on comprehensive policies, and only while the customer's vehicle is being repaired by an approved repairer. It is not provided when the vehicle is a total loss or stolen and not recovered.

## How long

The courtesy car is available for the duration of the repair, up to a maximum of 14 days. Extensions need team leader approval.
