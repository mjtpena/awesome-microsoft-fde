# Motor excess policy

Status: superseded
Version: 2
Groups: all-staff
Superseded by: Version 3

## Standard excess

The standard compulsory excess on a comprehensive motor policy is £200 per claim.

## Young and inexperienced drivers

An additional excess of £250 applies when the driver is under 25.

## Windscreen and glass

A windscreen replacement carries an excess of £75. Repairs carry no excess.
