# Underwriting flood risk referral policy

Status: current
Version: 3
Groups: all-staff
Owner: A. Novak, Underwriting policy manager

## When to refer

Refer a new home policy to an underwriter when the property is in flood risk zone 3, or has had a flood claim in the last ten years.

## What the underwriter decides

The underwriter decides whether to offer cover, apply a flood excess, or decline. Handlers do not quote for referred properties.
