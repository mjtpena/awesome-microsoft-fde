# Motor excess policy

Status: current
Version: 3
Groups: all-staff
Owner: A. Novak, Underwriting policy manager

## Standard excess

The standard compulsory excess on a comprehensive motor policy is £250 per claim. The customer pays the excess before any settlement or repair invoice is paid. Third party only claims carry no excess.

## Young and inexperienced drivers

An additional excess of £300 applies when the driver at the time of the incident is under 25, or has held a full licence for less than two years. The additional excess is added to the standard excess.

## Windscreen and glass

A windscreen repair carries no excess. A windscreen replacement carries an excess of £95. Glass claims do not affect the customer's no claims discount.

## Voluntary excess

Any voluntary excess the customer chose at inception is added to the compulsory excess. Check the policy schedule for the amount.
