# Property subsidence policy

Status: current
Version: 2
Groups: all-staff

## Excess

The excess for a subsidence, heave or landslip claim is £1,000.

## Evidence needed

Every subsidence claim needs a report from a chartered structural engineer before liability is decided. Handlers do not decide liability on subsidence claims themselves.

## Escalation

Refer every subsidence claim to the Property Technical team within two working days of first notification.
