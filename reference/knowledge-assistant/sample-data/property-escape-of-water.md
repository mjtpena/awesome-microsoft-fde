# Property escape of water policy

Status: current
Version: 4
Groups: all-staff

## Excess

The excess for an escape of water claim is £350. This replaces the standard buildings excess for these claims.

## Trace and access

The policy pays up to £5,000 to find and access the source of a leak, even if the leak itself caused no damage.

## Reporting

The customer must report the escape of water within 30 days of discovering it. Late notification is referred to a team leader, not declined automatically.
