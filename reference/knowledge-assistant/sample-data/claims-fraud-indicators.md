# Claims fraud indicators policy

Status: current
Version: 3
Groups: all-staff

## When to refer

Refer a claim to the Special Investigations Unit within 24 hours if you see two or more fraud indicators, such as a recently increased sum insured, inconsistent accounts of the incident, or reluctance to provide documents.

## What not to do

Do not tell the customer that the claim has been referred for investigation. Keep handling the claim as normal until the Special Investigations Unit contacts you.
