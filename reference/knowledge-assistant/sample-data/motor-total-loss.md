# Motor total loss policy

Status: current
Version: 2
Groups: all-staff

## When a vehicle is a total loss

Declare a vehicle a total loss when the estimated repair cost is more than 60% of its pre-accident market value, or when an engineer says it can't be repaired safely.

## Valuation and settlement

Use the market value on the day of the incident from an approved valuation guide. Pay the settlement, less the excess, within 10 working days of the customer accepting the offer.

## Salvage

The salvage category is set by the engineer. Tell the customer the category in writing before they decide whether to keep the vehicle.
