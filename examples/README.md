## The fictional engagement

**Harbourline Insurance** (fictional) has 4,000 staff. Claims handlers spend time searching three SharePoint sites and a file share for the current version of underwriting and claims policies. The sponsor, the Head of Claims Operations, wants handlers to ask questions in Teams and get answers that cite the policy they came from. The assistant only answers; it changes nothing.

What a good example shows:

- Every row filled in, or marked "doesn't apply" with a reason.
- Defences that say **how they're tested**, not just what they are.
- A named person accepting each residual risk.
- Decisions that point to the ADR where they were made.
